        Short Order CITY TYCOON

        One diner at a crossroads. Build the city around it, all the way to a metropolis.

        • Build with the bar at the bottom: pick Homes, Jobs, Food & shops or Services, choose a building, then click an empty block.
        • People move in when there are homes, jobs and food. They pay tax every minute, businesses earn from customers, and workers earn wages.
        • Keep them happy with parks, schools and hospitals. Hunger, unemployment and factory smoke make people leave.
        • Power: the street grid powers 8 buildings. After that, build power plants or the newest buildings go dark.
        • Grow: buy more land, level buildings up (click one), and unlock towers, malls and a stadium as your population rises.
        • Your diner at the centre still runs: zoom in to collect its cash, hustle staff and buy upgrades. Citizens walk over to eat there.
