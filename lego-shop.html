🧱 Welcome to Brick Shop Tycoon!

You just opened a tiny toy shop built out of bricks. Make it the biggest brick shop in town!

  1. Build a Cash Register so customers can pay.
  2. Build Product shelves — customers walk to them and buy stuff.
  3. Add Decorations, and build walls and floors with any brick you like in the Build tab. Nicer shops get more customers and bigger tips!
  4. Hire workers in the Staff tab. They help you earn more, but you pay them every minute.
  5. Earn money to unlock new bricks and expand your shop in the Upgrades tab.

Tip: click and drag to place lots of bricks. Right-click (or use the Sell tool) to remove one. Right-drag (or two fingers) spins the camera, scroll zooms.
