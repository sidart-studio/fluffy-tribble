🧱 Welcome to Brick Shop Tycoon!

You just opened a tiny toy shop built out of bricks. Make it the biggest brick shop in town!

  1. Build a Cash Register so customers can pay.
  2. Build Product shelves — customers walk to them and buy stuff.
  3. Add Decorations and Floor tiles to make your shop nicer. Nicer shops get more customers and bigger tips!
  4. Earn money to unlock new bricks and expand your shop in the Upgrades tab.

Tip: click and drag to place lots of bricks. Right-click (or use the Sell tool) to remove one.
