🎢 Coaster Park Tycoon

🎢 Coaster Park Tycoon

Build the most thrilling amusement park around. Lay paths from the entrance, place rides and shops next to them, and design your own roller coasters. Guests pay to enter and to ride; keep them fed, happy and thrilled to raise your park rating.

Building

  • Paths connect everything. Guests only walk on paths, and a ride or shop must touch a path to be used.
  • Rides & shops are placed from the toolbar. Click a ride later to set its ticket price and view its stats.
  • Coasters: choose Custom Coaster, then click (or drag) across neighbouring tiles to lay track. Use Q/E to change the height of the next piece (max one level per tile). Click the first piece again to close the circuit, then press Finish. Bigger drops and more turns mean more excitement.
  • Scenery raises the mood of guests walking nearby.

Controls

  • Pan: drag with the mouse (right/middle button, or left button with no build tool), or WASD / arrow keys. Zoom: mouse wheel.
  • Esc cancel tool · Space pause · 1–3 speed · Backspace undo track piece · Enter finish coaster · Del demolish tool

Goal

Reach a park rating of 750 with at least 150 guests in the park. Rides break down over time and are fixed automatically for a fee. Upkeep is charged at the end of every day. Your park autosaves in this browser.
